Each step is a campaign message, edited on Listmonk's normal campaign screen (content, attachments, archive…), but sent automatically to each person when they reach it. A step waits the given time after the previous email was sent (step 1: after the person enters the sequence). Opens and clicks are tracked per step under Sequences → Analytics.

  • Unsubscribe: Listmonk's {{ "{{ UnsubscribeURL }}" }} works as in any campaign (the default template already has it). It unsubscribes the person from this sequence's list.
  • "Send me the next email now" (optional): <a href="{{ "{{ SequenceNextURL }}" }}">Get the next email now</a>. Leave it out and the step just waits. Where people land afterwards: the sequence's Settings, or the message's Next email tab.
